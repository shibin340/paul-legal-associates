**Field definitions:** `sources` counts distinct generated pages with a link in `<main>` to the target, excluding the repeated site header/footer. Example sources show Home, Expertise, Finance or one relevant article when present, not an exhaustive list. `content depth` counts visible words and H2/H3 headings in the service's main article, excluding the related-service navigation. `insights out/in` counts links to insights from this page and insight pages linking back to it in generated `<main>` content. `primary query` is a mapping hypothesis, not observed rank or demand. `—` in Search Console metrics means the URL had no returned page row in this dataset; it does not prove zero impressions. `last update` refers to substantive page copy. `schema` records actual JSON-LD types, not eligibility or Google acceptance. All service rows have a telephone CTA and Contact path in the generated HTML. A live 200 check was conducted for Tier A; the three PR #19 pages were also verified after that deployment. Other pages were checked against the generated production build, not every live URL.
